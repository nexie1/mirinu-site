Open the app

Everything you watch and read, in one place.

Mirinu is a free app to track TV shows, movies, anime, manga, light novels, comics and novels, on the web and soon on Android. You tick off what you have watched, and Mirinu shows you what comes next.

Open Mirinu in your browser Android: coming soon to the Play Store.

Tick off an episode and the next one is waiting.

The Up next list gives, for each show, the next episode and how many episodes you have left. For a manga or a light novel, it is the next chapter.

On the page of a show you are watching, Mirinu works out, at your pace, around which day you will catch up with the latest episode. You can rate each title out of ten and keep a note on it for yourself.

Releases, day by day.

The Upcoming tab sorts the next episodes and chapters by date, as a list or a calendar. What is out and you have not watched yet stays at the top.

For manga, the date of the next chapter is an estimate, and Mirinu says so. On Android, a notification tells you when an episode comes out, with a button to mark it as watched, and a home screen widget shows the next three episodes to watch.

Bring over your TV Time or Trakt history.

If you come from another app, Mirinu reads the export file you give it and works out by itself where it comes from. It shows you what will change, and nothing is written until you confirm. The file stays on your device.

TV Time, Trakt, MyAnimeList, Letterboxd, Netflix.

Sort and filter your whole library.

Filter by type and by status, with the number of titles in each. Sort by latest activity, title, rating or date added. On a computer, the filters stay open on the left and your own lists in the sidebar.

Your year in numbers.

The year screen counts the time spent on your shows and movies, and the episodes, movies and chapters of the year, month by month, with your records. You can turn it into an image to share.

Your profile keeps the total since the start, your most-watched genres and a map of your activity, week after week.

Your library stays with you.

Mirinu does not ask for an account. Your library, ratings, notes and history are kept on your device.

To have the same library on your phone and in the browser, you link your devices with a code or a QR code, valid for two minutes. Everything is encrypted on the device before it leaves, with AES-256-GCM: the Mirinu relay only stores blocks it cannot read.

No ads and no trackers, in the app or on this page. Posters and dates come from TMDB and MangaUpdates, which only receive the title you search for.

Before you try it

Is Mirinu free?

Yes. Mirinu is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Mirinu works without an account, and without an email address. An optional account is planned for later, to get your library back if you lose your phone.

Can I bring over my TV Time history?

Yes. Ask TV Time for an export of your data, then pick the file in Mirinu, under “Import history”. Mirinu brings over watched episodes, rewatches, movies and the status of each show. Exports from Trakt, MyAnimeList, Letterboxd and Netflix come over the same way.

Where is my data?

On your device: in the app on Android, and in your browser's storage on the web version, without cookies. If you link two devices, your library goes through the Mirinu relay encrypted, and the relay erases what has not been used for 180 days.

Which devices does Mirinu work on?

In the browser, at app.mirinu.ch. The Android app is coming soon to the Play Store. There is no iPhone app for now.

In which languages?

French, English, German, Italian and Spanish.

A word on the price

I make Mirinu on my own, in Switzerland, and I use it every day.

For now, Mirinu is free. I will probably open donations later, and they will stay optional. If the community grows a lot, I might think about a subscription to pay for the servers and the rest.

What you keep in Mirinu stays on your device, and you can export it whenever you want.
