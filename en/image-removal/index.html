Remove an image

Updated on November 7, 2026.

The posters and photos shown in Mirinu belong to their rights holders. If one of them is yours and you no longer want it shown in Mirinu, we will remove it.

Where the images come from

The app loads most posters and photos from TMDB, TVmaze and MangaUpdates; Mirinu does not copy them to its servers. Posters proposed by Mirinu users are kept on Mirinu's server once they have been checked. Mirinu claims no rights to these images.

How to ask for removal

Write to contact@mirinu.ch with:

  • the title of the film, series or manga;
  • the image in question (its address or a screenshot);
  • who you are, and that you hold the rights or act on behalf of the rights holder.

What we do

We reply by email and remove the image from Mirinu. If it is a poster proposed by a user, we delete our copy and it can no longer be proposed. An image loaded from another site stays there: to have it removed there, please contact that site as well.
