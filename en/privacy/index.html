Privacy

Updated on October 29, 2026.

Translated from the French version, which prevails if the two differ.

Mirinu is a personal project by Marco, in Switzerland. He is responsible for the data described here. For any question: contact@mirinu.ch.

On your device

Mirinu has no account and asks for neither your name nor your email address. Your library, your ratings and your settings stay on your device: in the app on Android, in the browser's storage on app.mirinu.ch, without cookies. A file you import is read on the spot and sent nowhere.

On Android, if your phone's Google backup is on, it may copy your library, as it does for your other apps.

No ads, no trackers, no audience measurement.

What leaves your device

To show a title page or recognize the titles of an import, the app sends the title or number searched for to TMDB and MangaUpdates, directly or through the Mirinu relay. Images come from their servers, fonts and trailer thumbnails from Google's. These services see your IP address, like any website, and follow their own rules.

The “Help improve matching” setting, on at first, sends the relay the two numbers of an entry you pick during an import, with nothing that identifies you. You can turn it off in the settings.

Sync and security

If you link several devices, your library is encrypted on your device before it is sent to the relay. The relay only keeps data it cannot read, and the key never goes through it in the clear.

If you find a security flaw, write to contact@mirinu.ch before making it public.

Providers and countries

Cloudflare (United States, servers in several countries) runs the relay. On the website, manga and light novel searches and pages also go through Vercel (United States), without your IP address or anything that identifies you. GitHub (United States) hosts mirinu.ch and app.mirinu.ch, and keeps its own access logs. Infomaniak (Switzerland) hosts the contact@mirinu.ch mailbox. Cloudflare, Vercel and GitHub take part in the Data Privacy Framework, which Switzerland and the EU recognize for data sent to the United States.

Legal bases and retention

Mirinu follows the Swiss data protection act and, for people in the EU, the GDPR. Sync and showing title pages serve the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Matching and hosting rest on the legitimate interest in running the service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Mirinu does not keep your IP address. The relay erases a synced copy 180 days after its last sync. An email you send is kept while your request is handled.

Your rights

You can find out what Mirinu keeps about you, have it corrected or erased, receive it in a file, object to a processing or have it restricted. Write to contact@mirinu.ch: the answer comes within 30 days. Your library can be exported from the app, and the “Delete your data” page explains how to erase everything.

You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, the authority of your country.
