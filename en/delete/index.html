Delete your data

Updated on October 28, 2026.

Translated from the French version, which prevails if the two differ.

Your data is on your devices and, if you use sync or an account, in an encrypted copy on the Mirinu relay.

On Android

Uninstall Mirinu. To keep the app and start over: in Android's settings, open Apps, then Mirinu, Storage, and clear the storage. Exports you saved elsewhere remain: delete them yourself. A copy kept by your phone's Google backup is managed in that backup's settings.

In the browser

In your browser's settings, clear the site data of app.mirinu.ch. Your library disappears from that browser.

The sync copy

  1. On each linked device, open Sync, in Profile on a phone or in Settings on a computer.
  2. Choose “Stop sync on this device”.

Once no device syncs anymore, the relay erases the encrypted copy after 180 days. It cannot read it and does not know who it belongs to.

Your account

If you have an account, delete it in the app (Account and sync, Delete my account) or at app.mirinu.ch/#/supprimer, without installing the app. Your account and your encrypted library are erased from the relay 7 days after your request; until then, you can cancel. What is on your devices stays there.

A question

Write to contact@mirinu.ch. The Privacy page explains what Mirinu keeps and for how long.
